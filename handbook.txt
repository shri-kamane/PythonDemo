=====================================================
INDIAN ENGINEERING COLLEGE - STUDENT RULE BOOK
=====================================================

-----------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: ADMISSION
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Admission to all engineering diploma and degree programs is through the state Common Entrance Test (CET) or equivalent merit-based process.
2. Candidates must have passed 10th/12th standard (or equivalent) with minimum required percentage in Mathematics and Science.
3. Original documents (mark sheets, leaving certificate, caste/category certificate if applicable, domicile certificate) must be submitted at the time of admission.
4. Admission is provisional until document verification is completed by the Scrutiny Committee.
5. Lateral entry to second year is permitted for diploma holders as per institute quota and eligibility norms.
6. Fee payment must be completed within 7 days of seat confirmation, failing which the seat may be cancelled.

-----------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: EDUCATION / ACADEMICS
-----------------------------------------------------
1. The academic year is divided into two semesters: Odd (July-December) and Even (January-June).
2. Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory in each subject to be eligible to appear for semester exams.
3. Students must maintain a laboratory journal and submit assignments as per subject teacher's schedule.
4. Internal assessment (unit tests, assignments) carries 20% weightage; end-semester exam carries 80%.
5. Students failing in more than 3 subjects in a semester will not be promoted to the next semester (ATKT rules apply for 1-3 backlogs as per university norms).
6. Choice of elective subjects must be finalized within the first two weeks of the semester.

-----------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: ATTENDANCE
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Attendance is recorded for every lecture, tutorial, and practical session.
2. Students with attendance below 75% will be detained and not permitted to sit for exams, except on genuine medical grounds with proper documentation.
3. Medical leave beyond 3 days requires a certificate from a registered medical practitioner submitted to the class coordinator.
4. Attendance shortage condonation (up to 65%) may be granted by the Principal only in exceptional cases.
5. Biometric/manual attendance records are updated weekly and shared with parents/guardians.

-----------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: EXAMINATION
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Exams include Unit Tests, Practical/Oral exams, and Semester-End Theory exams as per university calendar.
2. Students must carry their hall ticket and college ID card to every exam.
3. Use of unfair means (copying, mobile phones, chits) will lead to disqualification and disciplinary action, including possible debarment.
4. Revaluation/photocopy applications must be submitted within 15 days of result declaration, with prescribed fee.
5. A student who fails to appear due to genuine reasons may apply for special exams as per university policy.

-----------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5: SPORTS
-----------------------------------------------------
1. The institute encourages participation in intramural and inter-collegiate sports (cricket, football, kabaddi, athletics, chess, etc.).
2. Sports quota admissions are subject to state-level sports certificates and trials.
3. Students representing the college at university/state/national level are granted attendance relaxation with prior approval.
4. Sports Day is organized annually; participation is open to all students.
5. Proper sports kit and ground rules must be followed; injuries must be reported to the sports department immediately.

-----------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6: EVENTS
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Technical festivals, cultural festivals, and annual gatherings are organized once every academic year.
2. Student clubs (coding club, robotics club, cultural committee) can propose events with faculty coordinator approval.
3. Participation in inter-college events requires a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the department.
4. Event budgets must be approved by the Student Council and Dean of Student Affairs before execution.
5. Ragging or any form of harassment during events is strictly prohibited and punishable under Anti-Ragging laws.

-----------------------------------------------------
SECTION 7: FEES
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Tuition fees are as per state government/AICTE approved fee structure, payable semester-wise.
2. Late fee payment attracts a penalty as notified by the accounts department.
3. Scholarship and fee reimbursement schemes (EBC, GOI, minority scholarships) are processed through the state scholarship portal.
4. Refund of fees on admission withdrawal follows AICTE/university refund policy timelines.
5. Hostel, library, and exam fees are separate from tuition and must be paid as per the notified schedule.
